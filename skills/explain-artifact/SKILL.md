---
name: explain-artifact
description: Prepare a user to explain a completed artifact through a short walkthrough and practice questions grounded in its actual files, during permitted preparation or development.
---

Inspect the actual artifact and its verification results. Explain its input-to-output flow, the responsibilities of key files, one important design choice, and a concrete limitation. Distinguish implemented behavior from proposals.

Check that the referenced evidence corresponds to the current files. Trace a claimed AI contribution to the actual delegation record, changed function, and verification result when those records exist. Identify a stale or unsupported claim rather than filling in missing history. Include a short restart exercise when requested: use the saved handoff to identify the next action without relying on the prior chat.

When practice is requested, ask one question at a time and wait for the user's answer before giving feedback. Useful questions ask why a structure was chosen, where a rule is implemented, how an error is handled, what evidence supports a result, and what would fail under changed conditions.

Evaluate explanations against the files rather than an idealized architecture. If a premise is false, identify the mismatch. Keep feedback focused on one factual correction and one improvement in clarity.

This skill is for permitted preparation or development. It does not authorize AI assistance during an assessment stage that prohibits it.
